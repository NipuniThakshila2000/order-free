I

Your guardian angels

Lenain’s physical and character correspondences use the civil birth date; the spirit correspondence uses the entered local clock time in twenty-minute intervals from midnight. These are abridged historical summaries, not verified effects. The portraits are illustrative.

Sources: Lenain, La Science Cabalistique (1823), Tables II–IV, pp. 28–38, for the three date and time correspondences; English edition, ch. VI, for the angel attributes. Portraits are illustrative.

Privacy Statement — Redeeming the Seasons

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are. This site is operated by WOWLife Church, Sri Lanka. Questions about your data: info@wowlife.lk.

What we collect and why.

Birth details and reflection results. When you use your personal reflection, we collect your given name(s) and family name, date of birth, the calendar you selected, time of birth (to the minute, hour only, or unknown), and place of birth. We use these to run the historical calculations shown on the page. We store these details and the resulting reflection (guardian angel, chart and year-by-year seasons) in your account so you can return to them without re-entering them. Without consent, the calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved.

Access application. To request full access we collect your full name, email address, phone number, the country you are accessing from, how long you have attended WOWLife Church, how you came across WOWLife teachings, and who recommended WOWLife Church to you. We use these to review your application and contact you about the outcome. Information about your connection with the church may indicate your religious beliefs; we collect it only with your explicit consent and use it only for this purpose.

Workshop registration. To register for Architecture of Freedom we collect your first and last name, email address and whether you will attend online or in person. We use these to run the event and send you joining details.

Account. If your application is approved we create a login for you and store the credentials needed to secure it.

Page metrics. We count page loads in daily totals to understand site use. These counters do not contain names, birth details, account IDs, or visitor identifiers.

What we do not do.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your birth details for marketing or profiling, and we do not share them with third parties except the hosting and email providers that run this site on our behalf under contract. We do not use historical symbolic results to make any decision about you.

How long we keep it.

Birth details and reflection results: until you delete them or close your account. Access applications: 12 months after a decision. Workshop registrations: 6 months after the event. Account records: while the account is active, then deleted within 30 days of closure.

Your rights.

You may ask us at any time to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent, and we will act on the request within one month. Withdrawing consent means your stored birth details and reflection will be deleted. Contact info@wowlife.lk. If you are in Sri Lanka you may also complain to the Data Protection Authority; if you are in the EU or UK, to your local supervisory authority.

Children.

This service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes.

We will post any changes here with a new date, and tell account holders by email if the changes are significant.
