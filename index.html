About the limits of this reading

Lenain assigns 20 March through 14 March to numbered date guardians; 15–19 March are five special days outside that numbered sequence. This application leaves 29 February unnumbered too; Lenain’s leap-year discussion allows more than one reading. Time slots use entered civil clock time, from 00:00–00:19 through 23:40–23:59; no conversion to solar time is implied. The art is illustrative.
